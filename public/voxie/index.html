Parents and carers

Voxie turns everyday jobs into a game children actually want to play. You set the jobs; they earn levels by doing them, and they look after a pixel buddy along the way.

Why the missions matter

Alongside puzzles and brain teasers, each day includes a "What would you do?" mission. These are real situations from the playground, group chats and online games, written to help children think about how they treat other people, and how they treat girls in particular. They practise noticing when something isn't OK, working out what's right, and they get the actual words to speak up.

  • "Stop" means stop, even when someone's laughing.
  • "Maybe" isn't a yes. Wait for a real yes.
  • Don't join in when someone's being laughed at online.
  • Back someone up, and tell a trusted adult.

There's no telling-off for a wrong answer. Every answer earns a little XP for having a go, and the explanation shows why the other choice is better, plus the words to use next time.

Safe by design

  • No real money, ever. XP is the only currency, and it only comes from missions.
  • No adverts.
  • Private. Friends only see each other's buddy, level and XP. No chat, no photos.
  • You're in charge of the account. You make your child's login, so we never ask for their email or age.
  • Mystery boxes and "Take a chance" can be switched off.
  • No timers if they don't suit your child. Timed missions can be turned off.

Your grown-up account

  • A tab for each child. Add them with a username and passcode you choose, and reset it if they forget.
  • Two grown-ups? Invite the other one with a code, so you can both look after your kids.
  • Set their tasks on the days they happen, like homework on school days or feeding the cat every day.
  • Choose their settings (timers, Mystery boxes, streaks) and decide whether they can change them themselves.
  • See how they're doing: tasks done, missions done, and how many questions they get right.
